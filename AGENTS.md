# em34xx — notes for coding agents

- Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` before opening or reviewing a pull request. Its
  rules apply to you and to the person you work for, including the required
  disclosure of tool, model and provider.
- When you review, check against `CONTRIBUTING.md`, state problems and
  expected behaviour rather than a design, and say what you ran versus what
  you only read.
- Project conventions, build and test commands are in `CLAUDE.md`.
